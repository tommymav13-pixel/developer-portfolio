in software engineering, cloud technologies, DevOps, and eventually
quantitative development.

Practical programming ability, projects, technical understanding, and
professional experience remain the primary development priorities.

## Certification Strategy

My certification strategy follows several principles:

1. Build practical foundations first.
2. Understand concepts rather than memorize exam questions.
3. Combine certification study with hands-on practice where possible.
4. Prefer certifications that support the current professional direction.
5. Avoid collecting certifications without corresponding technical ability.
6. Continue building projects alongside certification study.

The intended relationship is:

```text
Technical Study
      +
Practical Exercises
      +
Projects
      +
Certification
      ↓
Demonstrable Technical Competence
```

Certification therefore supports the broader development roadmap rather than
replacing it.
